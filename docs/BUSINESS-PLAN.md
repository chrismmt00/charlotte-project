## Who we are
Klyvora is a software and AI company based in Charlotte, NC. We build our own websites, applications, AI harnesses and restaurant systems, then reuse the underlying software to keep customer prices accessible. Our first market is Charlotte's independent businesses.

**Mission:** make useful business technology accessible. We help small businesses take their companies to the next level with affordable software that improves customer experiences, simplifies daily operations, and creates room for growth.

**Vision:** become a trusted technology partner for independent businesses, starting with Charlotte's and including Black-owned businesses, so owners can spend more time serving customers and growing their companies. That means helping a barber manage appointments, a restaurant connect its online and in-person operations, a retailer sell online, or a service company automate repetitive work.

**Our advantage** is delivering a working system and helping the owner use it. AI and our engineering skills make delivery faster and more affordable. Customers pay for understanding their business, completing the setup, connecting the necessary systems, training staff, and maintaining the result.

We write our own code and build reusable foundations. Each customer gets their branding, content, settings, and selected features. Substantial new functionality receives a separate quote.

## Who owns what
- **Klyvora owns what it builds:** the reusable platform, foundations, components, templates and tools, including improvements made during client projects. Package clients get a license to use it while their subscription is active.
- **Custom work belongs to the client:** anything built only for one client transfers to them on final payment. That covers a custom web application, the client-specific workflow and integrations of a custom AI harness, or a separately quoted feature. Klyvora platform code inside it stays Klyvora's and is licensed to the client.
- **The client always owns** their domain, branding, content, data, customer list and Stripe account. If they cancel, they keep all of it and get a full export of their content and data.
- **Payments stay with the owner.** Each client's checkout runs through their own Stripe account, which they control. Klyvora takes no cut of their sales; Klyvora's own fees are billed separately from Klyvora's Stripe account.

These terms go into the proposal/contract template, reviewed by a lawyer.

Track the three numbers separately:
1. **Signed sales:** agreed project and setup fees.
2. **Cash collected:** deposits and milestone payments actually received. Milestone billing leaves the last projects mid-payment at day 90, so cash runs roughly 85% of signed; signing about $24,000 gets cash to $20,000.
3. **Live recurring revenue:** active software, hosting, and support subscriptions. Hardware funds, finite installment payments, and variable usage charges don't count.

## Funding model and hardware
We grow on customer-funded equipment and milestone-based development.

- Customers buy approved hardware directly, or we collect its full cost before ordering.
- Software is paid against milestones: 50% at kickoff, 30% at a working demonstration, and 20% at acceptance. Monthly service billing begins at launch.
- Start with proven tablets and secure mounts. Consider imported kiosks after testing a sample and securing customer orders.
